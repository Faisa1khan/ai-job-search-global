# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Alex Doe operates with high technical ownership, engineering rigor, and a strong product focus. Combines deep domain execution in complex SaaS systems (payroll, compliance, workflow engines) with proactive architectural discipline (ADRs, state machines, modular testing) and cross-functional team collaboration.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Technical Ownership & Rigor | High | Drives architectural clarity, documentation (ADRs), and end-to-end reliability |
| Product & Business Empathy | High | Translates complex business rules into intuitive, reliable user workflows |
| Team Collaboration | High | Recipient of Acme Team Spirit Excellence Award; partners with Product, QA, and Backend |
| Continuous Craftsmanship | High | Proactive exploration of modern frontend architecture, AI tooling, and open-source tooling |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Architectural Discipline:** Uses state machines, feature modules, and ADRs to prevent chaotic complexity in evolving codebases.
- **Pragmatic Problem Solving:** Tackles intricate business logic (such as multi-country payroll calculations and statutory balancing) with clear mathematical and structural solutions.
- **Cross-Functional Partner:** Balances engineering excellence with business deadlines in high-paced SaaS environments.

## How You Work Best
- Autonomous ownership of features and architectural components from design to production.
- Product-driven environments that value code quality, testing (e2e, integration), and maintainability.
- Fast-paced, iterative SaaS teams where technical decisions are backed by reasoning and documentation.

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Deepening Backend Systems:** Expanding from frontend/full-stack (Supabase/PostgreSQL/Node) into large-scale distributed backend systems.
- **Public Developer Advocacy:** Increasing external open-source sharing and writing alongside internal product execution.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "High ownership", "product-minded engineer", "clean architecture", "scalable SaaS", "state machines", "design systems", "mentorship", "end-to-end feature delivery"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "Pure maintenance of legacy apps with no modernization mandate", "siloed development with no product involvement", "unstructured cowboy coding without tests/reviews"

## Management Style Preferences
- Clear strategic product vision with freedom on technical execution and architecture.
- Collaborative feedback culture with high standards for code quality and user experience.

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Emphasize high ownership in delivering mission-critical SaaS systems, legacy migrations, and disciplined architecture.
- **CV:** Highlight measurable technical delivery, multi-country domain complexity, and independent full-stack/AI projects.
- **Interviews:** Use STAR examples detailing complex payroll balancing, legacy migration without regressions, and building multi-tenant architectures.
- **Don't overstate:** Do not claim deep Kubernetes/DevOps infrastructure or non-web native mobile development beyond PWAs.
